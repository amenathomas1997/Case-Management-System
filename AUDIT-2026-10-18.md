# Code audit — October 18, 2026

**Version:** 4.26.0 (commit 1886fcb).

**Scope:** the whole system, about 96,000 lines:
- the website (141 pages, 86 helper files)
- the phone-app API
- the scheduled jobs
- the installer and updater
- the database schema and migrations
- the front-end scripts
- the iPhone app (25,000 lines)
- the Android app (8,000 lines)

**Method:**
- **Ten auditors**, each reading its own area from input to storage to output:
  - sign-in and access
  - the API
  - the public and private-link pages
  - the staff pages (in three parts)
  - billing, integrations, AI and updates
  - data, privacy and scheduled jobs
  - the iPhone app
  - the Android app
- **Reporting rule:** only real defects with a concrete failure scenario.
- **Verification:** findings marked **verified** were confirmed by re-reading the code independently of the auditor who found them. The rest were confirmed by their auditor's own reading, unless marked unconfirmed.
- **Read only:** nothing was changed during the audit.

## The short version

- **Nothing Critical.** Nothing an outsider can use to read client records.
- **Strong in the basics.** Sign-in, sessions, caseload limits on every page and API action, escaping, file handling, the updater's signatures, and token and backup encryption all held up.

The serious problems are narrower:
- two flaws in features added this week: the hand-over signing lock, and the lost-phone wipe
- one money bug: QuickBooks payments counted twice
- one records bug: a legal hold lost when clients are merged
- one action that crosses caseload limits: group cancel
- several smaller privacy, reliability and accessibility gaps

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| Critical | 0 |

## High

**H1. Signing in person on the website: after signing, Back reopens the staff page in the signer's hands.** *Verified.*
- `public/inc/bootstrap.php` `kiosk_guard()` lets through any request for `signatures.php?client=X&sign=Y`, or anything with `signed` in the address.
- Once the document is signed, that address shows the full staff Documents to sign page: every document, the send form, the withdraw buttons and the staff menu.
- A POST to `?signed=1` also runs the other handlers, so the signer could withdraw the client's other documents or email a document anywhere.
- **Fix:** while the device is handed over, allow only the hand-over page for a document still waiting, and only its own Sign or Hand-back post. Never render the staff page.
- **Also:** guard the public Help and Privacy pages and the keep-alive.

**H2. A payment recorded here and sent to QuickBooks comes back the next day as a second payment.** *Verified.*
- **Where:** `public/inc/qbo.php:253-275`.
- **Defect:** the "already seen" check looks only at the reference text "QuickBooks payment N". A payment typed here keeps its own reference (such as a cheque number), and its QuickBooks id is stored separately. The next sync therefore imports it again.
- **Scenario:** a $400 cheque on a $1,000 invoice shows as $800 paid. If the duplicate would overpay, every later sync of that invoice fails.
- **Fix:** treat a payment as seen when its stored QuickBooks id matches. Removing a payment that is already in QuickBooks needs a void there, not a silent delete.

**H3. A phone reported lost often never wipes.** *Verified (server).*
- **Where:** `public/api.php:422-431`, `inc/records_privacy.php:515`, the iPhone app's `API.swift` queue flush and document download.
- **Defect:** the wipe instruction is sent once only. After that the session simply looks expired.
- **How it gets lost:**
  - Resetting the password, deactivating the user, "sign out everywhere", 2FA reset and similar actions delete the wipe-pending row before the phone connects.
  - An already-expired session cannot be flagged.
  - The iPhone app ignores the wipe flag on its offline-queue and download requests.
- **Scenario:** a lost phone whose owner's password was also reset keeps its cached client lists, drafts and queued notes.
- **Fix:**
  - keep answering "wipe" until the app confirms
  - make the bulk sign-outs keep wipe-pending rows
  - allow flagging an expired session
  - check the flag on every iPhone request

**H4. Merging two clients drops the duplicate's legal hold.** *Verified.*
- **Where:** `public/client_merge.php`, `inc/client_merge.php`.
- **Defect:** the merge never looks at the hold.
- **Scenario:** a client on hold for a records request is merged into a record with no hold. All their records move onto it, the held row is deleted, and the records can later be purged.
- **Fix:** carry the hold onto the kept record (or refuse the merge until it is lifted), and say so on the confirmation page.

**H5. "Cancel the whole group" also cancels sessions of clients on other caseloads.** *Verified.*
- **Where:** `inc/session_changes.php` `session_cancel_group()`, reached from `schedule.php` and the API's `cancel_group`.
- **Defect:** only the session that was pressed is checked. Every session in the group is then cancelled, and those clients and instructors are emailed.
- **Fix:** for a caseload-limited user, refuse unless every member is theirs, or cancel only their own.

**H6. Android, on builds without a built-in server: editing the server address on Sign in deletes unsent offline work.**
- **Where:** `Api.kt` `switchServer`.
- **Defect:** this happens before the sign-in is even tried, and it includes signatures taken with no signal. Adding "https://", or changing capitals, counts as a different server.
- **Scope:** your own app has a built-in server and is not affected; other companies' builds may be.
- **Fix:** compare normalised addresses. Clear only after signing in to a server that really is different, and warn first when anything is waiting to be sent.
- **Same on iPhone (Low):** see L-iOS-4.

## Medium

**Access and accounts**
- **M1. Mailbox control alone takes over an account, even one with an authenticator app or passkeys.**
  - **Defect:** "Forgot your password" needs only the email link, and the emailed sign-in code is always accepted.
  - **Fix:** when an authenticator is set up, require it at sign-in, or at least after a password reset.
  - This may be a decision you want to make rather than a bug.
- **M2. Microsoft sign-in could match a guest to a staff account by email.** *Unconfirmed.*
  - **Defect:** the check relies on an `idp` claim that Microsoft's v2.0 tokens may not include.
  - **Fix:** require the `acct` claim, or match on a verified domain.

**Secrets**
- **M3. A staging copy keeps the production secrets** for Microsoft Graph, Twilio, QuickBooks, Zoom and Microsoft, as stored on Settings.
  - Calendar-invite emails bypass the staging "mail off" switch, so a tester can email real staff from the production mailbox.
  - **Fix:** the staging scrub removes every stored secret, and invites honour "mail off".
- **M4. The Twilio auth token is in every nightly backup and the off-site copy.** *Verified.*
  - **Defect:** the backup filter excludes names containing "secret" and "api_key", but the token is stored as `cfg.sms.auth_token`.
  - **Fix:** exclude every setting marked secret, and list them all in the restore instructions.

**Privacy**
- **M5. The full client export includes every incident report**, including ones the person may not see on the Incidents page or the timeline. *(Tasks assigned to other staff are included too, Low.)*
- **M6. Anyone who knows a client's email can repeatedly kill their portal link.** "Email me a new link" replaces the working link each time. Limits are per address only.
  - **Fix:** re-send the current link while it is valid, and limit requests per client.
- **M7. A phone number changed on the portal keeps the old texting consent**, so texts and session details go to the new number. Staff and app edits already clear it.
- **M8. A guardian's consent still counts as a release after the client turns 18**, for up to a year. Reports go to the counselor with no warning; the daily task about turning 18 says otherwise.
- **M9. Purging a client leaves their authorization emails** (client name and the full email) on Incoming authorizations.
- **M10. Automatic monthly reports skip the release check** that the manual send has. *Off by default.*

**Data correctness**
- **M11. Changing a custom field's type silently erases or blocks stored answers.** For example, text changed to date blanks the field on the next save.
  - **Fix:** refuse the change, or say how many answers it affects.
- **M12. Two people editing the same client: the later save overwrites the earlier one,** including reopening a case just closed or undoing a reassignment.
  - **Fix:** check the record has not changed since the form was opened.
- **M13. Merging drops fields not on its fill list:** VR case number, IPE dates, vision status, time zone, language and privacy notice.
- **M14. A closed referral can still be converted into a client** from a stale page.
- **M15. Booking in the apps: approving authorization warnings approves any warnings,** including new ones that appeared since and were never shown. The website ties approval to the warnings displayed.
- **M22. One authorization import file can create two tracks for the same authorization.**
  - **Defect:** the file's own duplicate check is case-sensitive, and there is no unique index on authorization numbers. "AUTH-1" and "auth-1" therefore both import, doubling the authorized hours and dollars that can be invoiced.
  - **Fix:** compare without case, and check again inside the import.
- **M16. Session reminders: when one recipient fails, the others are emailed again on every run.** A client with a bad address makes the instructor's reminder and an admin alert repeat until the session.

**Phone apps**
- **M17. iPhone: cancelling the session-signature sheet leaves the app unlocked in the client's hands,** unless they drew on the pad. The in-person documents view already locks always.
- **M18. iPhone: a crafted email address from the public referral form can redirect the Mail link** (such as `evil%40evil.com?@example.org`).
  - **Fix:** encode the address, as TrackDetailView already does.
- **M19. iPhone: the lost-phone wipe clears only the signed-in account's drafts and cache.** Another account's data on a shared phone stays.
- **M20. Android: turning the phone while "Sign" is in flight loses the result.** When staff take the phone back, the app withdraws a document that was actually signed.
- **M21. Android: every client record opened stays cached on the phone until sign-out,** all in one encrypted file that grows and slows the app.
  - **Fix:** limit the cache (for example 30 clients or 14 days), and keep it in its own file.

## Low

**Website — access and abuse**
- **Sign-in lockout:** limits are per IP across all accounts, so an office behind one address can lock itself out, and anyone can lock out a named account.
- **Sign-in codes:** the 5-codes cap can be exceeded with "Send a new code". Forgot-password timing reveals which emails are accounts.
- **Passkeys:** removing one doesn't end sessions started with it.
- **Health check:** one global rate limit lets anyone cause false downtime alerts.
- **Referral form:** `?submitted=1&fee=free` shows a convincing fake "received / free" page.
- **Portal uploads:** files attached to messages skip the 30-a-day cap.
- **Emails sent from the apps:** signature and release requests have no hourly cap.
- **Proxies:** behind a proxy, every per-IP limit becomes shared. *Unconfirmed for your server.*
- **Trash:** "Delete forever" ignores a legal hold, and the purge re-check misses a hold placed in the same moment.
- **Client list:** a link can change a user's saved client-list filter (GET changes a setting).

**Website — correctness**
- **QuickBooks:**
  - a zero balance from a void or credit memo is treated as paid, and syncing stops
  - an authorization email's counselor address is marked verified, with the box pre-ticked
- **Signatures:** in the API, logging hours keeps the entry when the drawn signature can't be read; the session form refuses in that case.
- **Offline replays:**
  - an offline check-in records the upload time, not the arrival time
  - a replayed follow-up shows as refused for ever
- **Duplicates:**
  - the phone survey saves twice on a double submit
  - the client import's in-file duplicate check is weaker than its check against existing records
- **Large imports:** a large client import can time out with no report; there is no row limit.
- **Texting log in the export:** the full client export includes the texting log, which is otherwise for administrators only.
- **Emails whose outcome is unknown:** a release or survey request is deleted when the email might still arrive.
- **Former counselors:** they still see report rows for clients no longer theirs.
- **Time zones:**
  - counselor portal times show no time zone
  - changing the organisation's time zone doesn't convert recorded times, despite the label
- **Monitoring:** when the database is down, the watchdog may have no address to alert (if the address is set only on Settings).
- **Migrations:** mig_3.0.36 is not safe to rerun once custom note types exist.
- **Agency scorecard:** misses renamed or aliased agency spellings in hours and clients.
- **Smaller:**
  - search treats `%` and `_` as wildcards
  - the templates page accepts arrays in two filters
  - the abandoned-device task can go to a deactivated account
  - the status hint on the edit page is wrong (every closure sends the survey)
  - an empty device check-in closes the follow-up

**Website — accessibility**
- **Errors not tied to their field or not summarised:**
  - moving a session (only the date field)
  - the task assignee
  - offices
  - the reservation item
  - the backup tick on Software update
- **Client import:** a missing or oversized file is not announced (no error summary).
- **Errors announced twice:** custom field errors are flashed and also on the field, with no summary.
- **Required fields not marked:** on the "Add an instrument" form.
- **Wrong language:** Spanish custom-question errors on the referral form are marked as English, so screen readers use an English voice.

**iPhone**
- **L-iOS-4. Changing the server address deletes unsent work before sign-in.** This only affects builds with no built-in server.
- **Offline queue:** entries never expire.
- **Siri:** the "Today's sessions" shortcut answers while the phone is locked (times only, no names).
- **Account switch:** the previous account's session reminders keep firing after a switch.
- **Guardian name:** the field offers the staff member's own name through AutoFill.
- **Widget:** low-contrast text.
- **Redirects:** API redirects aren't controlled. *Unconfirmed.*

**Android**
- **Uploads:** plain-text temporary copies can be left behind.
- **Full storage:** background sending can crash on resume.
- **Hand-back:**
  - it says "withdrawn" even when the withdrawal failed
  - "Sign here now" from the list withdraws a document that was also prepared on the phone

## What was checked and found sound

- **Sign-in:**
  - session cookies, rotation and expiry
  - CSRF everywhere
  - password storage and the timing-safe unknown-email path
  - one-time codes, TOTP replay protection
  - passkey checks (origin, challenge, signature, counter)
  - Microsoft sign-in bound by object id
  - password reset tokens
  - every staff page requires sign-in, and every admin page requires admin
- **Caseload limits:** every website page, report and API action that takes a record id checks the record belongs to the user's caseload. No way was found to open another caseload's record.
- **Output:** everything printed is escaped. Spreadsheets are protected against formulas. No SQL is built from user input without binding.
- **Files:** types, content and virus checks; random names outside the web folder; sent as downloads.
- **Tokens:** all private links are 192-bit random tokens with expiry. The counselor portal code is hashed, short-lived and rate-limited. The Twilio webhooks verify signatures, and OAuth checks its state.
- **Updater:** a pinned RSA signature, per-file hashes, safe paths, no downgrades, backup and rollback. The installer locks and removes itself.
- **Integrations and AI:**
  - tokens encrypted with a key outside the database
  - AI requests protected against reaching internal addresses, with names redacted and outputs escaped
  - incoming authorization emails trusted only when the sender authenticates
- **Billing:** cent-exact line amounts, row locks, and caps on payments and write-offs.
- **Phone apps:**
  - tokens in the Keychain or Keystore
  - nothing kept on a phone without a passcode or screen lock
  - complete file protection
  - no backups of client data
  - screenshots blocked (Android), the app-switcher cover (iPhone)
  - no logging of personal data
  - the in-person signing lock
  - labelled controls for VoiceOver and TalkBack
- **Purge and trash:** locks, file clean-up after commit, and redaction of the audit log, notifications and incidents.

4. **Decide:** M1 (whether the emailed code alone should be enough for staff who use an authenticator app). M2 needs a check against a real Microsoft sign-in.
