# Code audit, October 18, 2026 (third audit, after 4.28.0)

**Scope:** the whole system after 4.28.0. Ten auditors each took one part; the case pages were split among four helpers. Two auditors reviewed only the 4.28.0 changes. This time they read in full the screens and pages that earlier audits had only skimmed. Each finding was traced through the code, and the most important ones were reproduced on scratch databases.

**Result:** no High. There are 16 Medium findings after duplicates are merged: the app track-edit gap was found by four auditors. There are about 70 Low.

**What held up:**
- No caseload leak was found on any page.
- The fixes from both earlier audits held, apart from the gaps listed below.

## Medium

1. **P1 Microsoft auto-provisioning gives a deactivated person a new, active account.** The Microsoft identity lookup ignores deactivated rows. When the person's email has changed, provisioning then creates a new account (sso.php). Reproduced.
2. **P2 In-person signing on the website: "Sign in with Microsoft" or a stored passkey can bring the signer back into the staff session.** After the hand-over ends, the sign-in page offers both. The browser's Microsoft session can sign in with no password.
3. **P3 The app's track edit has no stale-form check (N9 was fixed on the website only).** A title-only save from either app reverses a colleague's authorization amendment and re-prices work. A restored iPhone draft makes it more likely. Reproduced.
4. **P4 The retry guard never runs for save_task, save_track and request_consent.** These run before the guard. A retry adds a second task, or emails the guardian twice and kills the first link. Reproduced.
5. **P5 The iPhone discards the retry key on "still being processed".** Following that advice saves a second copy.
6. **P6 Android: dialogs opened from unsaved state take the previous item's restored fields after a rotation.** Affected: Move, Correct hours, Record progress, and the Waitlist dialog. The waitlist case writes one client's status and notes onto another.
7. **P7 iPhone: Reload after a client conflict withdraws a texting agreement a colleague just recorded.** The phone change watcher compares against the first-loaded record.
8. **P8 Android: leaving a form while it saves can lose the entry.** The entry is neither sent nor kept offline: a note, hours with a signature, progress, an expense or a follow-up.
9. **P9 Staff bookings never check the instructor's other sessions.** An instructor can be double-booked silently, on the website and in the API.
10. **P10 "Make the meeting link" in another person's Zoom or Teams checks the legacy role column, not the admin permission.**
11. **P11 Merge then purge: the duplicate's audit entries keep the name, note subjects and guardian email after the purge.**
12. **P12 Offered times still book with an instructor who has since been deactivated or had the client transferred away.**
13. **P13 Private-pay and no-charge work is counted as VR agency work.** This affects the agency billing summary, the funder report and email, RSA-911, the scorecard and analytics. New tracks prefill "State VR".
14. **P14 Losing the key file can no longer be recovered as documented.** meeting_key() refuses to make a new key while any stored token exists. That blocks reconnecting QuickBooks and meetings, and new authenticator seeds are then stored unencrypted.
15. **P15 The staging scrub stops part way on most real sites.** Unique columns are all set to '[scrubbed]', so production data, password hashes and secrets stay in the staging database. Reproduced.
16. **P16 Restoring a backup silently switches the site to Central time.** restore.php defaults to America/Chicago.

### Audit 3, area diff-server (server-side changes v4.27.0 to v4.28.0)

#### [Medium] After the key file is lost, meeting_key() never makes a new one, so the documented recovery fails: QuickBooks and meeting accounts cannot be reconnected
- Where: public/inc/meetings.php:74-80 (meeting_key refusal), meetings.php:43-50 (meeting_key_dependents), public/qbo_connect.php:26 (qbo_store, no try/catch), public/meeting_connect.php:32-37, public/inc/security.php:221-229 (totp_secret_store fallback)
- What happens: dist/restore.php, cron/backup.sh and weekly_offsite.php all tell the administrator that a restore without meeting_tokens.key is recovered by running reset_2fa.php for each authenticator user and then connecting QuickBooks and the meeting accounts again. But meeting_key() now throws whenever the file is missing and meeting_key_dependents() > 0. That count includes every meeting_accounts row with tokens, for every user (deactivated staff included), plus the qbo_connection row and any 'v1:' seed, including seeds of inactive users. Nothing an administrator can do clears other staff members' meeting_accounts rows: only meeting_disconnect() for one's own account deletes them, and users.php does not. So the count stays above 0 and no key is ever made. The results:
  - The admin's QuickBooks reconnect reaches qbo_store(), then meeting_seal(), then meeting_key(), which throws an uncaught RuntimeException (a 500 on the OAuth return).
  - Each staff member's meeting connect fails with the wrong advice ("Ask an administrator to run Database upgrade").
  - Authenticators set up again after reset_2fa are stored as plain base32. totp_secret_store catches the exception and falls back silently, which undoes the 4.28.0 seed encryption.
  - Before 4.28.0 a fresh key was made and reconnecting worked.
- Fix: Count only the rows that matter for the refusal, or give admins a "forget stored connections" action that clears meeting_accounts and qbo_connection. Also catch the exception in qbo_connect.php and meeting_connect.php with the real reason, and make the restore text say exactly what must be cleared.
- Status: CONFIRMED (traced end to end)

#### [Medium] N9 fix incomplete: the app's track edit (save_track) still has no stale-form check
- Where: public/inc/api_parity.php:589-600 (save_track update path: locks the row, never compares orig or loaded_at); iOS ios/Sources/TrackDetailView.swift:1741-1745 sends every authorization field from the open form
- What happens: track_edit.php now refuses a save when the row changed after the form opened, but the app path does not. A colleague amends authorized hours or the rate on the website, for example from 20 to 30 hours with the amendment recorded. Staff then save a title or status change from an app form opened earlier, or from a restored draft ("Your unsaved changes to this track were restored"). The app sends the old authorized_hours, hourly_rate, mileage_rate and dates. save_track writes them back, records a reverse amendment, and re-prices unbilled and Draft-invoice work at the old rate. This is the same data loss N9 described, on the path the apps use.
- Fix: Do in save_track what save_client and save_worksite now do. Return updated_at (or a snapshot) with the track, accept loaded_at or orig, and answer 409 with the changed fields when the locked row differs. The apps then send it back.
- Status: CONFIRMED (traced)

#### [Low] Correcting a client's email re-sends the instructor's session reminder too
- Where: public/client_edit.php:286-289 and public/api.php:1763-1767 (UPDATE scheduled_sessions SET reminder_sent = 0, client_reminded_at = NULL); cron/session_reminders.php:78 and 116-117
- What happens: when a session has been fully reminded, the job sets reminder_sent = 1 and clears both client_reminded_at and staff_reminded_at. Changing the client's email resets reminder_sent to 0 for every upcoming session. On the next run, $clientDone and $staffDone are both false (both columns are NULL), so the instructor is emailed a second reminder for each already-reminded session. The reset also runs when the email is cleared, or changed to an unverified address the client will not be sent to. Then only the instructor's duplicate goes out.
- Fix: When resetting, set staff_reminded_at = NOW() for rows where reminder_sent was 1, so only the client side is redone. Skip the reset when the new address is empty or unverified.
- Status: CONFIRMED (traced)

#### [Medium] Android: dialogs opened from non-saved state get the previous item's restored fields (Move, Correct hours, Record progress, Waitlist)
- Where: android/.../ui/ScheduleScreen.kt:43,114 with NewSessionScreen.kt MoveDialog (`date`/`time`/`duration` now `rememberSaveable`, no key); TrackScreen.kt:68,73,197-198 with CorrectLogDialog (hours/miles/reason) and ProgressDialog (status/pct/pctEntered/note and `idem`); ReportsScreens.kt:104,118,125 WaitlistDialog (status/followup/notes)
- What happens: 4.28.0 changed these dialogs' fields from `remember` to `rememberSaveable` with no key. The state that opens each dialog (`moving`, `correcting`, `progressFor`, `chosen`) is still plain `remember`. Here is what happens if the phone rotates (the activity has no `configChanges`, so it is recreated) or the process dies while the dialog is open for item A. The dialog does not come back, but A's saved field values stay in the SaveableStateRegistry. Compose 1.7's `performSave` copies the unconsumed `restored` map forward, which I checked in the bytecode. The next time the same dialog opens for a different item B, the dialog sits at the same composite key, so it consumes A's values:
  - Move for session B is pre-filled with session A's date, time and length. Pressing Move moves B to A's slot.
  - Correct hours for log B shows log A's hours and miles.
  - Record progress for goal B shows goal A's status, percent and note. It also reuses A's retry key. If A's save had a lost reply, B's save gets A's stored reply back and is not saved, but the app says it was.
  - Waitlist for client B shows client A's status, follow-up date and notes. A's notes about one client are shown on and saved onto another client.
- Fix: make the opener saveable (store the id or JSON in `rememberSaveable`, as LendDialog, FollowupDialog and CompleteDialog do), or key each field with the item's id (`rememberSaveable(s.optInt("id"))`) so a stale value is never used for another item.
- Status: CONFIRMED (code traced, and the library's save and restore behaviour checked in runtime-saveable 1.7.0)

#### [Medium] iPhone: Reload after a client conflict withdraws a texting agreement a colleague just recorded
- Where: ios/Sources/ClientEditSheet.swift:279 (the phone's `onChange` compares with `existing`), :687-690 (reload), :581-587 (save)
- What happens: Someone else changes the client's phone from A to B and ticks "agreed to text messages". This person's save gets a 409 conflict, and they press Reload. `take(&phone…)` sets the phone to B, and `take(&smsConsent…)` sets it to true. Then SwiftUI runs the phone field's `onChange`, which compares B with `existing?.phone`. That is still the record as first opened (A), so it sets `smsConsent = false`. When they save, `agreed (false) != textingBase.agreed (true)`, so `sms_consent=false` is sent and the colleague's agreement is withdrawn under this person's name. With VoiceOver the switch flipping is easy to miss. Android is not affected because its phone check runs only when the person types.
- Fix: compare the phone with `base?.phone` in that `onChange`, or skip the reset while `reload()` is writing the fields.
- Status: CONFIRMED (traced)

#### [Low] iPhone: after Reload, status-change and closure fields follow the first-opened record, not the reloaded one

#### [Medium] Microsoft auto-provisioning gives a deactivated person a new, active account
- Where: public/sso.php:144-219. The identity lookup at line 144 is `WHERE entra_oid = ? AND is_active = 1`, and the "an account already has this email" check at line 177 looks only at the token's current email. Neither looks for a deactivated row that holds the same `oid`, so the auto-provision branch at line 190 runs. `users.entra_oid` has no unique key (schema_full.sql:1643, KEY only).
- What happens:
  1. A staff member linked to Microsoft (entra_oid X) is deactivated on Users.
  2. Their Microsoft account is still a member of the tenant, but its email/UPN now differs from the CMS row. For example, their name changed, or the administrator changed the departed account's email (which also clears entra_oid).
  3. They choose "Sign in with Microsoft".
  4. No active row has oid X, and no row has the new email, so a new `users` row is created in the auto-provision group with entra_oid X. They are signed straight in, with that group's access to client records. Deactivation is bypassed.
- Reproduced with the test harness (synthetic tenant, acct 0): a deactivated user with oid "oid-left-1" and email old.name@…, then a Microsoft return with the same oid and email new.name@…. Result: a new row (id 3, is_active 1, the provision group), audited as `user.provision` and then `login`.
- Fix: before matching by email or provisioning, look up `entra_oid = ?` with no is_active filter. If the oid belongs to a deactivated account, refuse and audit it. Consider a unique index on entra_oid.
- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)

#### [Medium] In-person signing hand-over: "Sign in with Microsoft" (or an unlocked password-manager passkey) puts the signer straight back into the staff session
- Where: public/inc/bootstrap.php:642-649 (`kiosk_end` sends the device to login.php). On that page, public/login.php:178-180 offers the SSO button and :173 the passkey button. public/sso.php has no hand-over check, and `?start=1` and the callback reach `establish_user_session()` (line 237).
- What happens:
  1. The staff member hands the device over (signatures.php `&sign=`).
  2. The signer goes anywhere else, or opens a new tab. `kiosk_end()` signs the staff member out and shows the sign-in page, as designed: "the staff member signs in again to carry on".
  3. On that page the signer chooses "Sign in with Microsoft". The staff browser still holds the staff member's Microsoft session (common, because staff use the same tenant for Outlook and Teams), so Microsoft returns a code without a password, or after a single account-picker click.
  4. sso.php then creates a full staff session for the signer, who now has every record on that caseload. Neither the hand-over nor Microsoft asks for anything the signer lacks.
- The passkey button works the same way when the staff member's passkey sits in a browser password manager that is unlocked.
- Fix: keep a "this browser was handed over" marker through `kiosk_end()` (for example a short-lived cookie). While it is set:
  - send `prompt=login` (or `max_age=0`, then check `auth_time`) on the SSO request
  - refuse passkey sign-in, so only password plus code or authenticator works
  - clear the marker after a full sign-in.
- Status: CONFIRMED (code path traced; it depends on a live Microsoft browser session, which is outside the code)
